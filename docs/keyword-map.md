# Keyword-to-page map

This map was written during the build. Search volumes and keyword difficulty were **not measured**: no keyword-research tool was available, and no volume or difficulty figures appear here. Each "intent" is a qualitative judgement from how the query is worded and what a searcher would likely want to do next.

| Page (URL) | Primary keyword hypothesis | Related terms | Search intent | Why this page is distinct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/best-french-reading-apps/` | best French reading apps | French reader apps, LingQ vs Readlang | Commercial comparison | Compares reading-focused tools only |
| `/best-apps-to-learn-french/` | best apps to learn French | which app to learn French, French learning apps | Commercial, broad buying guide | Groups apps by need, not one ranking |
| `/duolingo-alternatives-for-french/` | Duolingo alternatives for French | Duolingo alternatives, better than Duolingo for French | Commercial, considering a switch | Explains why learners outgrow Duolingo; does not compare head-to-head |
| `/duolingo-vs-sorlio/` | Duolingo vs Sorlio | Duolingo or Sorlio, which is better for French | Direct comparison, late-stage | Head-to-head comparison with conditional verdict |
| `/how-to-learn-french/` | how to learn French | learn French by yourself, French study plan | Informational, method | Broad guide with study plans; the most general page |
| `/learn-french-by-reading/` | learn French through reading | reading method, comprehensible input French | Informational, method | Focused on reading method only |

## Overlap decisions

- **Duolingo alternatives vs Duolingo vs Sorlio.** Both mention Duolingo. The alternatives page explains *why* and *what kinds* of alternative exist. The comparison page is a direct, conditional head-to-head. They serve different points in the same decision.
- **Best reading apps vs best apps to learn French.** The reading-apps page covers one category in depth. The broader page summarises all categories and links to the reading page for depth.
- **How to learn French vs learn French by reading.** The method guide covers all skills in one plan. The reading page goes deep on one method. Both link to each other.
- **A2 practice vs news.** The A2 page is a fixed passage with exercises. The news page is a method for using news at any level.

## Not targeted (deliberately)

- Brand-name pages for competitors, such as "LingQ review", until pricing and features can be verified.
- Near-duplicate pages such as "best French apps 2026" or "best French apps for beginners" as separate URLs. The existing pages cover those intents within one URL each.
