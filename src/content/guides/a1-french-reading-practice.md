## About this passage

This is an original text written for this guide. It uses the present tense, very common words and short sentences, which is what A1 reading looks like. It has been checked for grammar by us, but it has not been reviewed by a teacher.

## Introduction in English

Nina is a student in Bordeaux. She lives with her cat. The text describes her day: breakfast, travel, work and evening. Read it once for the story, then answer the questions without looking back.

## The passage

**Ma journée**

Je m'appelle Nina. J'ai vingt ans. J'habite à Bordeaux avec mon chat. Il s'appelle Mimi.

Le matin, je mange du pain et je bois du thé. Ensuite, je vais à l'université en bus. L'après-midi, je travaille à la bibliothèque. Il y a beaucoup de livres.

Le soir, je rentre à la maison. Je regarde un film et je mange une pizza. Je suis contente.

## Grammar notes

**Je + verb.** Most verbs in the passage start with *je*. In the present tense, verbs ending in *-er* take *-e* with *je*: *j'habite*, *je travaille*, *je regarde*, *je rentre*, *je mange*.

**J'ai and je suis.** *Avoir* (to have) is used for age: *j'ai vingt ans*. *Être* (to be) is used for how you feel: *je suis contente*.

**Du.** *Du pain* means "some bread". *Du* is used before a masculine noun for an unspecified amount.

**Il y a.** This means "there is" or "there are": *il y a beaucoup de livres*.

**Contente.** The *e* at the end shows that Nina is a woman. A man would say *content*.

## Questions

1. What is the girl's name?
2. How old is she?
3. Where does she live?
4. What is the cat's name?
5. What does she eat in the morning?
6. How does she go to university?
7. Where does she work in the afternoon?
8. What does she do in the evening?

## Answers

1. Nina.
2. Twenty (*vingt ans*).
3. In Bordeaux.
4. Mimi.
5. Bread, and she drinks tea (*du pain*, *du thé*).
6. By bus (*en bus*).
7. At the library (*à la bibliothèque*).
8. She watches a film and eats a pizza.

## Practice tasks

- **Copy the passage** by hand once, slowly. Notice the accents.
- **Replace Nina's details** with your own: your name, age, city and one daily activity.
- **Read it aloud** three times, slowing down on words you find hard.
- **Cover the English** in the vocabulary table and recall each meaning.
- **Write four sentences** about your morning, using *je* plus a verb.
