## About this practice text

This passage is original and was written for this guide. It is not taken from a published article or book. It aims at B1 on the CEFR scale, where a reader can follow the main points of clear texts on familiar topics. It has not been reviewed by a native-speaker teacher. If you are preparing for an exam, check it with a teacher or a course.

## Introduction in English

Julien has just started a new job in a city he did not know. In this passage he describes his first month: the flat he found, the colleagues who helped him, and a decision he is still thinking about. Read it once for the story, then answer the questions without looking back.

## The passage

**Un mois à Nantes**

Il y a un mois, Julien a quitté son village pour s'installer à Nantes. Il avait peur de ne pas trouver ses marques, mais la ville l'a vite séduit. L'appartement qu'il a trouvé est petit, cependant il est proche de son bureau, ce qui lui permet de rentrer à pied chaque soir.

Ses collègues l'ont accueilli avec beaucoup de gentillesse. Sophie, qui travaille au service comptable depuis cinq ans, lui a expliqué les habitudes de l'équipe. Elle lui a aussi conseillé un petit restaurant où l'on mange très bien pour un prix raisonnable. Julien lui en est reconnaissant, même si, au début, il ne comprenait pas toujours les blagues de ses collègues.

Le plus difficile reste la décision qu'il doit prendre. Son responsable lui a proposé de partir six mois à Lille pour un projet important. Julien aimerait que sa famille soit plus contente de sa carrière, mais il craint de perdre le rythme qu'il vient de trouver à Nantes. Il faudrait qu'il réponde avant vendredi, et il n'est pas sûr de savoir ce qui est le mieux pour lui.

## Grammar in the passage

**Relative pronouns.** *Il est proche de son bureau, ce qui lui permet de rentrer à pied* uses *ce qui* to refer back to the whole idea of being close to the office. *Une collègue qui travaille au service comptable* would use *qui* for a person who is the subject of the clause.

**The subjunctive after a verb of need.** *Il faudrait qu'il réponde avant vendredi* uses the subjunctive *réponde* after *il faudrait que*. The subjunctive appears after expressions of necessity, wish and fear. *Julien aimerait que sa famille soit plus contente* is another example after *aimer que*.

**Pronouns *lui* and *en*.** *Elle lui a aussi conseillé* means "she also advised him". *Julien lui en est reconnaissant* uses *en* to refer back to the advice, meaning "he is grateful for it".

## Comprehension questions

1. How long ago did Julien move to Nantes?
2. Why does he like his flat?
3. How long has Sophie worked in the accounts department?
4. What does Sophie recommend?
5. What is Julien's difficulty with his colleagues at first?
6. What has his manager offered him?
7. Why is Julien hesitant about accepting?
8. When does he need to reply?

## Answers and explanations

1. A month ago (*il y a un mois*).
2. It is close to his office, so he can walk home in the evening. The passage says it is small, but the location is the advantage.
3. Five years (*depuis cinq ans*).
4. A small restaurant with good food at a reasonable price.
5. He did not always understand his colleagues' jokes.
6. Six months in Lille on an important project.
7. He fears losing the rhythm he has found in Nantes, and he is not sure what is best for him.
8. By Friday (*avant vendredi*).

## Further practice

- **Summarise the passage** in five French sentences, without copying phrases.
- **Find three uses of the subjunctive** in another text and note the trigger word before each.
- **Rewrite the decision paragraph** in the first person, as Julien would speak to a friend.
- **Make a list of the idioms** you did not know, and use each in a sentence about your own week.
- **Time yourself:** a B1 passage of this length should take a few minutes on a second reading.
