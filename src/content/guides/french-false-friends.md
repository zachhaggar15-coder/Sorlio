## Why they matter

English and French share thousands of words, and that is a gift to readers. But a small group look the same and mean something quite different. They are the words most likely to make you misread a sentence while feeling perfectly confident. The reliable defence is to know the common ones, and to be suspicious when a word that "looks English" makes the sentence strange.

## Three patterns you can learn

1. **Meaning shift.** *Actuellement*, *éventuellement* and *ancien* are English-looking time words that mean something else. They pop up in news constantly.
2. **Place and thing.** *Librairie*, *coin*, *location* and *cabinet* name places or things in everyday life, so misreading them changes the whole scene.
3. **Verb traps.** *Attendre*, *demander*, *assister* and *rester* are common verbs, so the mistake repeats until you fix it.

## A reading test

Read this and notice where English would mislead you:

> *Actuellement, mon ancien collègue travaille dans une librairie près du coin de la rue. Il attend le car tous les matins.*

A false-friend reading might give "Actually, my ancient colleague works in a library near the coin of the street. He attends the car every morning." The correct reading is: "Currently, my former colleague works in a bookshop near the corner of the street. He waits for the coach every morning." The first is nonsense, which is the clue that something is wrong.

## How to catch them while you read

- **Trust your sense of oddness.** If a sentence feels strange, test the English-looking word first.
- **Check the grammar.** A word you think is a noun may be a verb, or the other way round.
- **Keep a list of your own.** When one catches you, write it down with the sentence. Your traps may differ from this list.
- **Use the context.** *Large* in *une rue large* cannot be "large" in a way that makes sense.

## A quick exercise

Give the real meaning of the underlined word, then check.

1. *Il est sensible au bruit.*
2. *Elle assiste à la réunion.*
3. *Je vais à la librairie.*
4. *Il habite actuellement ici.*

**Answers:** 1. Sensitive to noise. 2. She is attending the meeting. 3. I'm going to the bookshop. 4. He currently lives here.
