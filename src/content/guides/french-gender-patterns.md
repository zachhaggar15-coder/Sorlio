## Why gender matters when you read

Every French noun is masculine or feminine, and that choice ripples through a sentence. It decides whether you read *un* or *une*, *le* or *la*, *beau* or *belle*, and it tells you which noun a pronoun like *elle* or *il* is pointing back to. When you read, you do not have to produce the right gender. You do have to notice it, because it is one of the best clues to what refers to what.

The good news is that gender is not random. Many nouns follow patterns, and your eye can pick them up quickly.

## Endings that suggest feminine

| Ending | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *-tion*, *-sion* | *la nation*, *la décision*, *la télévision* | Very reliable |
| *-té* | *la liberté*, *la beauté*, *la sécurité* | Exceptions: *l'été*, *le côté* |
| *-ée* | *la journée*, *l'idée*, *la soirée* | Exceptions: *le musée*, *le lycée* |
| *-ure* | *la voiture*, *la nature*, *la culture* | Strong pattern |
| *-ance*, *-ence* | *la chance*, *la patience*, *la science* | Exception: *le silence* |
| *-ie* | *la vie*, *la boulangerie*, *la géographie* | Exception: *le génie* |
| *-ette* | *la fourchette*, *la cigarette* | Often small things |

## Endings that suggest masculine

| Ending | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *-ment* | *le gouvernement*, *le moment*, *le logement* | Very reliable |
| *-age* | *le voyage*, *le village*, *le fromage* | Exceptions: *la page*, *la plage*, *l'image*, *la cage* |
| *-eau* | *le bateau*, *le chapeau*, *le bureau* | Exceptions: *l'eau*, *la peau* |
| *-isme* | *le tourisme*, *le réalisme* | Very reliable |
| *-oir* | *le miroir*, *le couloir*, *le soir* | Strong pattern |
| *-al* | *le journal*, *l'animal*, *le cheval* | Reliable |

A note on that last row: before a vowel, *le* and *la* both become *l'*, so *l'animal* hides its gender. That is one reason to learn the article with the noun.

## Where the patterns break

Some endings are mixed. Nouns in *-eur* are often masculine (*le moteur*, *le chanteur*), but many abstract ones are feminine (*la peur*, *la couleur*, *la chaleur*, *la douleur*). Nouns in *-e* are more often feminine, but *le livre*, *le problème*, *le système* and *le stade* are masculine. Treat these as hints, not guarantees.

## Pairs that change meaning

A few words are spelled the same and differ only in gender:

| Masculine | Feminine |
| --- | --- |
| *le tour* (a turn, a trip) | *la tour* (a tower) |
| *le poste* (a job, a set) | *la poste* (the post office) |
| *le voile* (a veil) | *la voile* (a sail) |
| *le mode* (a mode, a method) | *la mode* (fashion) |
| *le livre* (a book) | *la livre* (a pound) |

When a sentence puts *un* or *une* before such a word, the article settles the meaning.

## Habits that help

1. **Learn the article with the noun.** Say *une table*, not *table*. The pair travels as one unit.
2. **Use adjectives as clues.** If you see *grande* or *petite* with a noun, it is probably feminine.
3. **Watch possessives.** *Mon* and *ma*, *ton* and *ta* give the game away, except before a vowel, where *mon* is used for both.
4. **Group by ending.** When you save a new word, write down its ending pattern, and notice when it breaks.
5. **Do not stop to analyse.** In extensive reading, note the gender when it is obvious and move on.

## A quick exercise

Guess the gender from the ending, then check.

1. *la / le* ____ nation
2. *la / le* ____ gouvernement
3. *la / le* ____ voyage
4. *la / le* ____ liberté
5. *la / le* ____ bateau
6. *la / le* ____ journée

**Answers:** 1. *la nation* (feminine, *-tion*). 2. *le gouvernement* (masculine, *-ment*). 3. *le voyage* (masculine, *-age*). 4. *la liberté* (feminine, *-té*). 5. *le bateau* (masculine, *-eau*). 6. *la journée* (feminine, *-ée*).

## Reading with gender in mind

Take this sentence:

> *La directrice a présenté le nouveau projet. Elle l'a trouvé très intéressant.*

*Elle* points back to *la directrice* (feminine), and *l'* stands for *le projet* (masculine). Without gender, you could not tell who found what interesting. This is how gender helps you read: it tracks the sentence for you.
