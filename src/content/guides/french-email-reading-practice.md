## Before you read

Informal emails often mix a reason, a request and an alternative in a few lines. This original fictional message is intended as approximately B1 reading practice; it is not a calibrated exam text. Read it once to understand what has changed. On a second reading, find the details that would help the recipient respond.

## Le message

**Objet : Notre visite de samedi**

Salut Emma,

Je suis désolée, mais je ne pourrai pas te retrouver à la gare à dix heures samedi. Ma sœur doit travailler plus tôt que prévu et m'a demandé de garder son fils pendant la matinée. Je peux être libre vers midi, si cela te convient.

On pourrait déjeuner près de la gare avant d'aller à l'exposition. Elle ferme à dix-huit heures, donc nous aurions encore le temps de la voir. J'ai déjà acheté nos billets pour quatorze heures ; ils sont valables toute la journée, d'après le musée.

Si tu préfères commencer sans moi, je te rejoindrai devant l'entrée à quatorze heures. En revanche, préviens-moi avant vendredi soir pour que je sache où te retrouver. Je peux aussi te rembourser ton billet si tu décides de rentrer plus tôt.

À bientôt,  
Léa

## Questions

1. Why can Léa not meet Emma at ten?
2. When does Léa expect to be free?
3. What does she suggest doing before the exhibition?
4. What has she already bought, and what does she say about their validity?
5. Where will they meet if Emma starts without her?
6. By when should Emma tell Léa her preference, and why?
7. Is Emma obliged to attend? Which sentence supports your answer?

## Answers

1. She has agreed to look after her sister's son during the morning because her sister must work earlier than planned.
2. Around midday: *vers midi* is approximate.
3. Have lunch near the station.
4. Their tickets for 14:00; according to the museum, they are valid all day. This is a statement within a fictional message, not a rule about real tickets.
5. In front of the entrance at 14:00.
6. Before Friday evening, so Léa knows where to meet her.
7. No. Léa offers to reimburse Emma's ticket if she decides to return early. That offer makes an alternative explicit.

## Language that changes the meaning

*Plus tôt que prévu* means earlier than expected. It explains why the original plan has become difficult; it does not tell us the exact starting time. *Si cela te convient* asks whether the proposed time suits the other person. *On pourrait* is a suggestion, softer than a fixed arrangement. *En revanche* introduces a contrasting condition, and *pour que je sache* expresses the purpose of the request: so that Léa knows.

The future *je te rejoindrai* means “I will meet you there”. The preceding *si tu préfères* is in the present, even though the possible action is in the future. Notice also *je ne pourrai pas*: the negative surrounds the future verb and changes the whole arrangement.

## Respond in French

Write two sentences accepting lunch at midday, or propose a different plan. For example: *Midi me convient. On peut déjeuner près de la gare avant l'exposition.* Then check that your reply answers the practical question: where and when will you meet?
